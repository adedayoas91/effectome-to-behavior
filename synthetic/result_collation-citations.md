## Limits that must remain in the report

Independent units are raw-process seeds. Directions are averaged within seed, and methods, windows, trials, Monte Carlo rollouts and reruns do not multiply the independent-seed count. A 2,000-resample bootstrap with three seeds has sparse empirical support and weakly supported coverage; it remains descriptive. With three nonzero pairs, even unanimous signs have minimum two-sided raw p-value `2/2^3=0.25`; this is binomial arithmetic, not a new confidence-interval result. The mechanism study has one independent seed and receives no repeated-seed p-value or confidence-interval claim. Complete execution, synthetic recovery, independent methodological confirmation and biological necessity remain separate claims.

The SciPy function page has no asserted publication date; the standalone BibTeX entry uses `year={n.d.}` and retains the actual access date in its note. This avoids treating access year as publication year.
