# Statistical appendix

Each seed contributes one paired MAE gain per scenario, delay and history. The driver test counts gains strictly above 0.005 and each control counts gains strictly below it. The maximum of the three exact one-sided binomial p-values defines the joint p-value. Holm adjustment covers the full 22-cell family. Seed bootstrap intervals use 20,000 resamples and are pointwise descriptive intervals. Origins and histories overlap within seeds.
