# Statistical appendix

The protocol fixed the primary 2D joint readout, intervention candidates, budgets, and three seed pools. Calibration uses the rank-19 order statistic of per-seed null maxima, with a floor of 0.10. The final null statistic is the fraction of 60 independently generated null cases with any nomination. The one-sided exact 95% upper bound is per paired dynamics and readout combination.

Secondary comparisons were specified before inspecting test outputs. They compare driver end-to-end MAE for the primary encoder versus 2D PCA, 2D PLS, and the equally sized behavior-only network separately for known support and all four native methods. Absolute improvements are baseline MAE minus joint MAE. Relative improvement divides that difference of means by baseline mean MAE. Exact two-sided sign tests use the 20 paired seeds within each method, dropping exact ties. The null is equal probability of either sign across independent paired seeds. No normality assumption is required. Holm adjustment covers all 15 sign tests and allows method dependence. Paired percentile bootstrap intervals use 20,000 resamples of entire seeds and express exploratory precision for mean differences. They are pointwise intervals and are separate from the family-adjusted sign tests. Within-seed outcomes and native methods are dependent. Descriptive standard deviations are across raw process seeds.
