# Statistical appendix

Five process seeds are the independent development units. Scenario, method, endpoint and candidate comparisons are paired. The 1D/full-decoder comparison is an algebraic identity audited by replay, so no independent-method hypothesis test is claimed.

Descriptive statistics use seed means and sample SD (ddof=1). Approximate percentile 95% bootstrap intervals in statistics.json enumerate all 3125 ordered five-seed resamples. Small-n intervals have no asserted confirmatory coverage and omit selection uncertainty.

All five PCA dimensions versus the fixed score are exploratory contrasts. Exact two-sided sign tests require independent seed-pair signs under P(positive)=0.5, without normality or a normality pretest. Holm correction covers all five contrasts. These tests do not make the algebraically constructed score an independent learning method.
