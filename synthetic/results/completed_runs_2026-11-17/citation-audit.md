## Retrieval and metadata limits

Efron's BibTeX was retrieved from [Crossref's transformation endpoint](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1214/aos/1176344552/transform/application/x-bibtex); author initials, year, volume, issue, pages and DOI were retained. Only the key, layout and page dash were normalized, and the optional month macro was omitted. The publisher DOI resolved, but its landing page returned an anti-bot wrapper during direct retrieval; publisher-deposited registry metadata and the original scan supply the bibliographic and abstract check.

Holm's BibTeX was retrieved from [BibBase](https://bibbase.org/network/publication/holm-asimplesequentiallyrejectivemultipletestprocedure-1979) and checked against the publisher issue and library record. The optional abstract and previous user's access date were removed and the key normalized. The often-copied DOI `10.2307/4615733` returned **404 at both doi.org and Crossref**. It is omitted; the verified stable JSTOR URL identifies the article. The article landing was script-only. The [indexed institutional scan](https://www.ime.usp.br/~abe/lista/pdf4R8xPVzCnX.pdf) supplied the original abstract, although direct PDF retrieval returned 403. The familywise-error interpretation is corroborated by the [official R documentation](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/RHOME/library/stats/html/p.adjust.html).

## Scope of the reported statistics

- Framework outcomes are paired by independent raw-process seed. The two-sided exact sign test uses positive/nonzero paired-difference counts under a sign probability of one half; tie handling and nonzero counts must remain explicit. It tests a sign tendency, not the magnitude of a mean improvement. No normality pretest is used. Holm adjustment covers all 16 declared contrasts, including nonsignificant outcomes.
- Framework bootstrap intervals use 2,000 seed resamples. With three independent seeds, empirical support is sparse and coverage is weakly supported; more resamples do not add independent observations. Even unanimous signs with three nonzero pairs have minimum two-sided raw p-value `2/2^3 = 0.25`. This is binomial arithmetic, not a new coverage result.
- Driver summaries retain their stored 2,000-resample descriptive seed bootstrap. Directions are averaged within seed; methods, origins and rollouts do not multiply seed replication. Its null bound remains a separate one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson procedure per method under the fixed nomination rule.
- The mechanism study has one independent seed and remains descriptive: this collation supplies no repeated-seed p-value or confidence-interval claim for it. Execution, synthetic recovery, independent methodological confirmation and biological necessity remain separate claims.
