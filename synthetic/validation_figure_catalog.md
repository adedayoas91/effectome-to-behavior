# Four-CSL validation figures

Only conventional c-GC/c-GC* and fastC-GC/fastC-GC* appear in current simulation
results. The four methods form one related discovery family.

Figures01–02 show repeated-seed predictive gains and numerical availability.
Means, sample SD and seed-bootstrap95% intervals retain complete planned
seed denominators. Failed or incomplete contrasts cannot acquire significance
by being omitted. Inspect available/planned counts before interpreting a gain.

Figure03 displays generating circuits and raw activity. Its linear recurrence
is known simulator truth, not an inferred VAR estimator result. Opposite-sign
rotations can share snapshot marginals and binary support; the covariance
identity is a construction, not evidence that support estimators distinguish them.

Figures04–05, when present, read the original saved first declared comparison.
They show the shared chart, one observed transition matrix, baseline and four
predicted matrices, followed by transition-specific feature omissions. No
stochastic estimator is rerun to recreate figures and no favorable seed is
substituted. Missing detailed evidence leaves those panels unavailable.

Table01 reports exact mean±sample SD and available/planned seeds. Table02 maps
control design to its validation question. Numeric states are local to a fitted
chart; positive predictive importance does not establish intervention effects.
The intervention-defined study and its own acceptance figures remain separate.
